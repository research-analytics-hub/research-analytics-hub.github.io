REACH, the Research Evaluation & Analytics Capacity Hub, brings together research
administrators, analysts, librarians and researchers who work on research analytics
and responsible research assessment. Most of what the community does happens through
its programs and the annual Summit, both described on the
[REACH website](https://reach.uky.edu/).

This site has a narrower job. It lists code and data that community members have
published openly, so that an analyst facing a familiar problem can find someone who
has already worked through it. Each project lives in its own GitHub repository, owned
by the people who maintain it. We keep the index.

## What you'll find here

Projects range from a single cleaning script to a full analysis pipeline with
documentation. Some are data sets, such as crosswalks between classification schemes or
synthetic records built for teaching. Others are written guides that walk through a method step by step.
Every listing names a maintainer you can contact, and every project page shows when
its repository was last updated, so you can judge how current it is before you rely
on it.

## What we check, and what we don't

Before a project is listed, a REACH maintainer confirms that the repository is public and
licensed and that it holds no restricted or identifiable data. We don't
audit the code or certify results. Treat a listing as a pointer to work worth looking
at, and read the repository's own documentation before you use it.

## Getting in touch

Questions about a specific project go to its maintainers, usually through the
repository's issue tracker on GitHub. Questions about this site, or a listing you think
should change, can go to the
[site's issue tracker](https://github.com/research-analytics-hub/research-analytics-hub.github.io/issues).
